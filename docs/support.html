Frequently asked

Which headphones work?

AirPods Pro (all generations), AirPods 3, AirPods 4, AirPods Max and Beats Fit Pro. AirPods 1 and 2 have no motion sensor, so Nape cannot track with them.

Nape says "Waiting for AirPods" although they are connected.

Make sure at least one earbud is in your ear; the sensor only reports when worn. If it still doesn't connect, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness and check that Nape is allowed.

Does it work when the app is closed?

It works in the background (other apps open, screen locked). It cannot work if you swipe Nape away from the app switcher or restart the phone; iOS gives no app access to headphone motion without a running process. Open Nape once and leave it in the background.

The angle looks wrong.

Tap "Set upright pose" while sitting straight and looking ahead. Nape measures every tilt from that position. Redo it if you change how the earbuds sit.

Why did tracking stop after a phone call?

It shouldn't; Nape resumes automatically after audio interruptions. If it does stop, please report it with your iOS version and headphone model.

What is "average load"?

An estimate of the weight your neck carried, averaged over the minutes you spent leaning. See the science section. It is a trend to lower, not a medical measurement.

Hangi kulaklıklar çalışır?

AirPods Pro (tüm nesiller), AirPods 3, AirPods 4, AirPods Max ve Beats Fit Pro. AirPods 1 ve 2'de hareket sensörü yoktur.

Uygulama kapalıyken çalışır mı?

Arka planda çalışır (başka uygulama açık, ekran kilitli). Uygulamayı yukarı kaydırıp kapatırsanız ya da telefonu yeniden başlatırsanız çalışamaz; iOS, çalışan bir süreç olmadan kulaklık hareket verisine erişim vermez. Nape'i bir kez açıp arka planda bırakın.

Açı yanlış görünüyor.

Dik oturup karşıya bakarken "Dik duruşu ayarla"ya dokunun. Kulaklıkların oturuşu değişirse yeniden yapın.
